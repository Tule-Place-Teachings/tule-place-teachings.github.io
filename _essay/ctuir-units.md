The CTUIR Department of Natural Resources created two other units that are very complementary to the content of Tule Place Teachings. Their Wy-Kan-Ush-Pum (Salmon People) unit explores several essential concepts surrounding first foods relationships in much more detail than was possible here. Their Rock Imagery unit provides an excellent overview of the history, significance, and meanings of these features.

Downloadable versions of these two units will be available right here very soon!
